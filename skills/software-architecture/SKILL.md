# Software Architecture

- Design systems for maintainability, scalability, reliability, and simplicity.
- Favor clear boundaries, separation of concerns, and low coupling.
- Optimize for long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.
- Keep business logic independent from infrastructure and external services.
- Prefer composition over inheritance.
- Design interfaces around business capabilities rather than implementation details.
- Minimize duplication while avoiding premature abstraction.
- Make dependencies explicit and unidirectional whenever possible.
- Keep modules cohesive and responsibilities well-defined.
- Prefer simple solutions unless complexity is justified by clear requirements.
- Consider operational concerns such as observability, testing, deployment, and security during design.
- Evaluate tradeoffs rather than pursuing architectural purity.
- Optimize for team productivity and system evolvability.
- Ensure architecture decisions align with business goals and user needs.
